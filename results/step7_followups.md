## 2. Multiscale: resolution, cost and the capture effect

**Units.** The detector probes the cycle frequency α_p = k(1 + â)/P_samp at α_p ± δ, δ = 0.25/N_seg (`discriminator.predicted_alpha`, `loop_wf.py`): δ is in α (cycles/sample), not in a. In units of a the probes sit at ±δ P_samp/k = ±2.44e-3 and the main lobe of C(â) has nulls at about ±P_samp/(k N_seg) = ±9.8e-3 (measured pull-in ±1.05e-2, Part B). The two paths differ by Δa = 1e-4, 1 % of the lobe half-width — the same ratio as Δα = Δa/20 = 5e-6 vs 1/N_seg = 4.9e-4. Within one block (N_c = 24000) the two lines are Δα·N_c = 0.12 cycles apart: unresolved by the block as well.

**Capture effect.** Unresolved, the two harmonics act as one line 1 + r e^{jψ(t)} (r = amplitude ratio of the mean-power harmonics = p2/p1; ψ turns once per 1/Δα = 2e5 samples ≈ 8.3 blocks). Its instantaneous frequency, in units of Δa above a1, is r(r + cos ψ)/(1 + r² + 2r cos ψ): per block the lock point swings between −r/(1−r) and r/(1+r); averaged over ψ it is exactly 0 for r < 1 (the stronger path, path 1) and 1 for r > 1 — not the power-weighted p2. For r = 1 the sum passes through zero and the average is undefined (the loop wanders). The loop (time constant 100 blocks) averages over ~12 turns of ψ.
