**Normalisation factor G.** With h_p = A_p0 c_p e^{−j2π f_Tx τ_p}/2 (Σ|c_p|² = 1), the exact mean received signal power over one period is P_ch = ⟨Σ_pq h_p h_q* R_X(t − τ_p, t − τ_q)⟩_t, R_X(t1, t2) = E[X(t1) X*(t2)]; P_ref is the same for one path with c = 1. G = P_ref / P_ch, and the channel's amplitude A_p0 is multiplied by √G (the received power by G), so every channel has the single-path received power and the same effective SNR. Without cross terms (paths uncorrelated: integer delay differences, or random phases on average) P_ch = P_ref and G = 1; G > 1 means the paths' cross terms are negative on average (partial cancellation: the received power fell and had to be raised), G < 1 that they add constructively.
