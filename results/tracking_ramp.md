Lag (bias-free) = (ε_up − ε_down)/2, bias = (ε_up + ε_down)/2. Std: within each direction, pooled. RMSE: per direction (√ of the mean of the two directions' MSE) and bias-removed (ε minus the bias estimate). Prediction: lag −β/g, std (σ_e/K_d)·√(g/(2−g)), RMSE √((β/g)² + (σ_e/K_d)² g/(2−g)), with σ_e/K_d = 1.60e-5 (noiseless) and 4.59e-5 (WP) from Step 4. Std errors from the spread of the R realisation means.
