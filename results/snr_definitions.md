# SNR definitions and the 10 % symbol-error working point

Generated by `study_snr_definitions.py`. All in terms of σ_D²/P_n (the SNR used so far), single path, 22-Jan channel (|A0|² = 1.2960).

## Definitions

| SNR definition | in terms of σ_D²/P_n | offset (dB) |
|---|---|---|
| per received sample, active part | |A0|²/4 · σ_D²/P_n | -4.89 |
| per received sample, average over symbol + GI | |A0|²/4 · N_is/N_sym · σ_D²/P_n | -5.86 |
| per data symbol after DFnT, Es/N0 | |A0|²/4 · σ_D²/P_n | -4.89 |
| Eb/N0, BPSK | = Es/N0 | -4.89 |
| Eb/N0, QPSK | Es/N0 / 2 | -7.90 |

Derivation: `signal_model.rx` gives Y = h e^{j2πΔf t} X + n with h = A_p0 e^{−j2πf_Tx τ_p0}/2 (the e^{jπ/4} of Δφ is the one in X); X = ΦD with Φ unitary, so E|X|² = σ_D² on the active part and the DFnT (Φ^H) leaves the noise white with variance P_n (`_BlockNoise`: E|n|² = P_n).

Checks from the simulation: noiseless demodulation reproduces D (max |D̂ − D| = 7.7e-06); measured active-part signal power 0.3240 σ_D² (expected 0.3240); measured noise power / P_n = 1.0004 (expected 1).
