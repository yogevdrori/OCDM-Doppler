## Prediction from the Fourier coefficients of the on/off pattern

|c_k| = |sin(π k d)|/(π k), d = 256/320 = 0.8. With Δ = {0} the cost at the harmonic is ∝ |c_k|²; δ is fixed in α, so K_d (in a) ∝ k/P_samp and the pull-in ±P_samp/(k N_seg). If the detector noise is set by the CAF estimation noise, σ_e/K_d ∝ P_samp/(k |c_k|) where the signal dominates (high SNR) and ∝ P_samp/(k |c_k|²) where the noise dominates.
